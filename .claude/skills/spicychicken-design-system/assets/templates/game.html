In play, the coin plate shows how far the next coin star is, and the meter's states differ in words, in the glyph's shape and in the fill's thickness; colour only repeats them. The cinematic preview draws letterbox bars, hides every HUD plate except the caption and the skip control, and centres a title card across the stage, above the captions. Skip is a real button; Escape still pauses.

What is shared, and what the game owns

Shared: the stage, HUD slots, plates, crosshair, timer, measure, count, stars, milestones, meter, hint, controls legend, the capture layer and cinematic moments (letterbox, captions, skip, title card), plus the optional capture lifecycle and truncating formatters in sc-game.js.

Owned by the game: physics, snow, levels, coin rules, saves, held-input clearing, and every colour on the canvas. Snow whites, timber, gold coins and a purple key are world materials, never tokens.
